## Scheduling and precedence

- A confirmed row-specific trigger overrides every calendar cadence. Review it immediately, including an affected risk outside the scheduled population. An unconfirmed report is a verification task, not a confirmed breach.
- Weekly = last completed review plus 7 calendar days; daily during an active event = plus 1 day. Monthly, quarterly, semiannual and annual = plus 1, 3, 6 and 12 calendar months, clamped to the final day of the target month. These are explicit scheduling conventions because the workbook does not specify calendar boundaries. Apply a provided organizational calendar instead when available and record the substitution.
- Credit transmission has two separate clocks: monthly metrics and quarterly deep dive. Critical dependency has quarterly and event-driven obligations. Long-horizon risks remain in the quarterly full-register review even when their dedicated weak-signal review is semiannual. Never let a slower layer suppress a faster overlapping obligation.
- Missing history means baseline review required, not proof that a review is overdue. Missing event status means Unknown, not no active event. Only completed work advances the applicable completion date; partial research does not complete an internal operational review.
- Reuse the same evidence across overlapping layers while keeping layer-specific decisions and completion states. Deduplicate tasks by risk ID, indicator and observation period.

## Decisions and change control

- Keep each stable ER- ID. Additions use the next unused ID. Merges/retirements retain traceable tombstones or change records; never reuse IDs or renumber after sorting. Keep the seven supplied categories unless an explicit user-approved taxonomy change applies.
- A standalone risk needs an independent emergence mechanism, exposure pathway, consequence, evidence base and distinction from existing rows. A topic, technology, driver or opportunity alone is insufficient. Record retain/add/merge/retire/reclassify proposals and rationale in an append-only challenge history.
- One dominant Evidence Type per risk: Observed Trend, Fact, Expert Assessment, Forecast, or Weak Signal / Speculative Hypothesis. Evidence Type, qualitative Evidence Strength and numerical maturity/reliability are different judgments; never mechanically convert one to another.
- Velocity means time from an identified trigger to first material organizational impact. Do not substitute adoption timing, technology arrival, task speed or time until the trigger. Unsupported fine timing stays Unknown. Preserve historical broad bands and existing qualitative priorities in the baseline; record evidence-backed proposed changes and approval status separately. In particular, carry forward the ER-7 band conflict and inherited September 18 numerical/link review until explicitly resolved by evidence and an authorized decision.
- Immediate requires High/Medium evidence, 0–12-month velocity and current action relevance. Apply the remaining priority and category-rank rules from the methodology. Where tie-breaking is undefined, preserve existing tied order; place new tied entries after existing ones in ascending numeric ER- ID order, and label this administrative tie convention. Do not replace qualitative priority with numerical scores.
- A direct causal link must pass Causality, Evidence, Directness, Distinctness and Applicability. Record Confirmed, Excluded or Insufficient evidence for each reviewed candidate and the decisive reason. Distinct compatible onward paths are required for cascade uplift; shared drivers, duplicates and cycles do not qualify. An incomplete review cannot produce a false zero confirmed-link score.
- Do not change model weights, normalize the optional fine-velocity reference into new scores or calculate internal/company priority without authorized inputs and methodology. If documenting a proposed numerical update, recompute only affected dependencies under the existing formula and label it proposed. Missing inputs remain missing.
- A proposed change is not approval. Record suggested reviewer roles from the framework. Do not claim an ERM, Legal/Compliance, risk-owner or independent-challenge decision occurred without evidence. Separate research completion, management approval and operational action completion.
